Desert Safari

Art of Dunes Camp offers camel safaris in some of the most beautiful and remote areas of the Jaisalmer Desert. Our experienced guides will take you on an unforgettable journey through this incredible landscape. You'll have the opportunity to experience traditional Bedouin life, stay overnight in a desert camp, and see some of the most amazing sunrises and sunsets you'll ever witness. So if you're looking for an adventure that you'll never forget, book a camel safari with The King of Desert Safari today!

Jeep Safari

A Jeep desert safari in Jaisalmer is an exciting and adventure-filled experience. There are many companies offering tours in Jaisalmer, and it is important to research before booking a tour. The driver of Night Jeeps are well-trained and have good experience of the Desert terrain. On a Night Jeep Safari, you can visit the forts, resorts, and inner towns and villages around the area. Quad Biking

Quad Bike Desert Safari in Jaisalmer is a popular adventure activity in Jaisalmer. It includes a Safety Briefing conducted by experienced Desert Safari guides who will go over the safety guidelines and provide all the necessary equipment. An all-terrain vehicle (ATV) is a four-wheeler that travels on low-pressure tires, with a seat that is straddled by the operator, and handlebars for steering control.

Parasailing

The Jaisalmer is a popular destination for Parasailing in India. This sport requires no special prerequisites and anyone (except minor children and people with special cases) can enjoy it with loads of fun. The Parasail wing is tied to a vehicle through a tow rope and the vehicle drives off with good speed to send the person into the air. After a fixed distance stipulated by the agency, the vehicle slowly decreases its speed, and a safe landing is ensured. The experts in Parasailing present at Sam Village will provide instructions and ensure safety.

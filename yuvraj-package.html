Deluxe Tent

The Deluxe Tents at Art of Dunes Camp Jaisalmer offer a comfortable and memorable stay in the Thar Desert. Each tent features an attached toilet and bathroom, a double occupied bed, table and chair, split AC, and geyser. The tents provide a peaceful private space with a comfortable verandah sitting area where guests can relax and enjoy the desert surroundings. Experience the beauty of the Thar Desert along with traditional Rajasthani hospitality, desert activities, beautiful sunset views, and comfortable accommodation at Art of Dunes Camp.
  • Traditional welcome with Aarti & Tikka
  • Welcome drink (non-alcoholic) on arrival
  • Accommodation in Deluxe Tent for a couple with 01 Kid below yrs
  • One Camel Safari in the evening. (Two pax for each camel)
  • Experience on sand dunes in a Jeep safari
  • Sunset View on Dunes After Sunset Come Back to Camp
  • Evening Tea and Bonfire with cultural program & veg. snacks
  • Buffet Veg. Dinner
  • DJ Group dance
  • Rajasthani Dance
  • 24 Hours running Water
  • Buffet Breakfast (Fixed Menu)
  • Complimentary 02 Bottles of Mineral Water on the day of arrival
  • After Check Out from Camp Will proceed to visit our hotel

Art of Dunes Camp

Experience the Soul of the Thar

Welcome to Art of Dunes, a beautiful desert camp where the timeless charm of Rajasthan meets the comfort of a memorable desert stay. Set amidst the golden dunes of Jaisalmer, we invite you to slow down among peaceful landscapes, warm Rajasthani hospitality, traditional culture, and unforgettable evenings beneath the open sky.

From relaxing in thoughtfully designed tents to enjoying Rajasthani folk performances, desert adventures, and magical sunset moments, every experience at Art of Dunes is created to help guests discover the beauty and spirit of the Thar Desert.

A stay where golden dunes, royal hospitality, and Rajasthan's vibrant culture come together.
